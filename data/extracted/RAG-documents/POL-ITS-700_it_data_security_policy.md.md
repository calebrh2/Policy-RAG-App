# Meridian Analytics — IT & Data Security Policy

Meridian Analytics — IT & Data Security Policy

Document ID: POL-ITS-700

Version: 1.0

Publication date: 1 March 2026

Owner: IT Security

## 1. Purpose and Scope
This policy establishes the security standards all employees, contractors, and interns must follow when using Meridian Analytics systems, devices, and data. It applies to company-issued equipment, personal devices used for work purposes, and any third-party service that stores or processes company data. It works alongside the Remote Work Policy (POL-RW-100), which sets out additional security requirements specific to remote and hybrid work arrangements.

## 2. Data Classification
Meridian classifies data into three tiers. Public data may be shared externally without restriction, such as marketing material. Internal data, including most day-to-day business documents, should not be shared outside the company without a business reason. Confidential data, including customer records, financial data, employee personal information, and security credentials, requires the highest level of protection and may only be accessed by employees whose role requires it. Employees should assume data is Internal by default unless it is explicitly marked otherwise.

## 3. Device and Account Security
All company-issued laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled, which is configured by default before a device is issued. Employees must not disable encryption, antivirus software, or endpoint monitoring tools installed by IT Security. Screens must be locked whenever a device is unattended, even briefly, and automatic screen lock must be set to activate after no more than 5 minutes of inactivity.

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for all company accounts, including email, VPN, and internal applications. Employees may not share login credentials with any other person, including colleagues, managers, or IT staff. IT Security will never ask an employee for their password; any such request should be treated as a phishing attempt and reported immediately.

## 4. Network Access
Access to internal systems from outside a Meridian office requires an active VPN connection. Employees must not access company systems over public or unsecured Wi-Fi networks without the VPN enabled. Personal devices used to access company email or internal tools must first be enrolled in Meridian's Mobile Device Management (MDM) system, consistent with the requirement described in the Remote Work Policy.

## 5. Software and Third-Party Tools
Employees may only install software approved by IT on company-issued devices. Requests for new software, including browser extensions and productivity tools, must be submitted through the IT service desk and reviewed for security risk before approval. Use of unapproved cloud storage or file-sharing services to store or transmit company data, including Confidential or Internal data, is prohibited, regardless of convenience.

## 6. Incident Reporting
Any suspected security incident — including a lost or stolen device, a suspicious email, unauthorized account access, or accidental disclosure of Confidential data — must be reported to IT Security within 1 hour of discovery. Prompt reporting is not subject to disciplinary action even where the employee's own action contributed to the incident; delayed reporting, by contrast, may result in disciplinary consequences given the increased risk to the company.

## 7. Data Retention and Disposal
Confidential data should not be retained longer than necessary for its business purpose. Physical documents containing Confidential data must be shredded, not placed in regular recycling or trash. Digital files containing Confidential data must be deleted through IT-approved secure deletion tools when no longer needed, rather than a standard file deletion, which does not fully remove underlying data.

## 8. Acceptable Use
Company systems are provided for business use. Limited personal use of email or internet access is permitted provided it does not interfere with job performance, consume significant system resources, or violate any other provision of this policy. Employees should have no expectation of privacy when using company systems, which may be monitored for security and compliance purposes consistent with applicable law.

## 9. Security Awareness Training
All employees must complete IT Security's annual security awareness training by the deadline communicated each year. New hires must complete an initial training module within their first 2 weeks of employment. Failure to complete required training by the deadline may result in temporary suspension of system access until training is completed.

## 10. Policy Violations
Violations of this policy, including sharing credentials, disabling security controls, or failing to report an incident promptly, may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination, and may be referred to Legal where warranted.

## 11. Questions
Questions about this policy, or requests to classify a specific dataset, should be directed to IT Security.
