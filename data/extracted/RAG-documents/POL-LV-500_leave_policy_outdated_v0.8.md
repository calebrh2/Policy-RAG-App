# Meridian Analytics — Leave Policy

Meridian Analytics — Leave Policy

Document ID: POL-LV-500

Version: 0.8

Publication date: 1 April 2023

Owner: Human Resources

## 1. Purpose and Scope
This policy summarizes the paid and unpaid leave entitlements available to full-time employees at Meridian Analytics. It applies to all full-time employees at Meridian's offices.

## 2. Vacation / Paid Time Off
Employees accrue Paid Time Off at a rate of 15 days per year, credited monthly. Unused PTO does not roll over into the following calendar year and is forfeited on December 31. Requests must be submitted to the employee's manager by email at least 5 business days in advance, regardless of length.

## 3. Sick Leave
Employees receive 5 days of sick leave per year, credited at the start of the leave year. Sick leave may be used for the employee's own illness or to care for an immediate family member who is ill. Unused sick leave does not roll over.

## 4. Parental Leave
Employees who have completed at least twelve months of continuous service are eligible for up to 8 weeks of parental leave following the birth or adoption of a child, paid at 50% of base salary for the full duration. Employees should notify their manager and Human Resources at least 60 days before an anticipated start date.

## 5. Bereavement Leave
Employees may take up to 3 paid days of bereavement leave following the death of an immediate family member. Additional unpaid leave may be requested and is evaluated at manager discretion.

## 6. Jury Duty
Employees summoned for jury duty receive paid leave for the duration of the service upon presenting the summons to Human Resources.

## 7. Military Leave
Leave for military service is provided in accordance with applicable law. Employees should contact Human Resources as soon as orders are received.

## 8. Unpaid Personal Leave
Employees with at least three years of continuous service may request up to 60 days of unpaid personal leave for personal or family circumstances, subject to manager approval and at least 30 days' advance notice. Benefits continuation during unpaid leave is handled on a case-by-case basis with Human Resources.

## 9. Requesting Leave
All leave requests should be submitted to the employee's manager by email, who will notify Human Resources for recordkeeping purposes.

## 10. Questions
Direct any questions about eligibility or entitlement under this policy to Human Resources.
