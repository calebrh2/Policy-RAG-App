Section-first: split using Markdown ## and ### headings.
Preferred size: 100–450 tokens.
Hard maximum: 500 tokens.

Small sections: merge only with closely related content; keep short complete rules, definitions, targets, or tables.
Large sections: split by subsection, numbered-item groups, paragraphs, then sentences as a last resort.

Tables: keep whole with title, units, headers, rows, and footnotes; split only large tables between rows.

Overlap: no sliding overlap. Repeat document title, section path, and relevant list/table introduction.

Metadata: store document, section path, pages, version, status, content type, and chunk ID.

Versions: current and outdated Carbon Plan chunks stay separate; filter to current unless the query is historical or comparative.

Non-policy content: mark cover details, approvals, sign-offs, and company boilerplate as non-searchable.
