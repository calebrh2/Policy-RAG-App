# Meridian Analytics — Health & Benefits Policy

Meridian Analytics — Health & Benefits Policy

Document ID: POL-HB-600

Version: 1.0

Publication date: 1 March 2026

Owner: People Operations

## 1. Purpose and Scope
This policy summarizes the health and welfare benefits available to eligible employees at Meridian Analytics, including medical, dental, and vision coverage, wellness programs, and related enrollment rules. It applies to all full-time employees; part-time employees working at least 20 hours per week are eligible for a subset of benefits as described below. Specific plan documents issued by Meridian's insurance carriers govern detailed coverage terms; this policy governs eligibility, enrollment, and administration.

## 2. Eligibility
Full-time employees become eligible for benefits on their first day of employment. Part-time employees working 20 or more hours per week become eligible after 60 days of continuous service. Eligible dependents include a spouse or domestic partner and children up to age 26, regardless of student or marital status.

## 3. Medical, Dental, and Vision Coverage
Meridian offers a choice of two medical plans: a Preferred Provider Organization (PPO) plan and a High-Deductible Health Plan (HDHP) paired with a Health Savings Account (HSA). The company contributes 80% of the premium for employee-only coverage and 60% of the premium for coverage that includes dependents, under either plan. Dental and vision coverage are offered as separate, optional plans, fully employee-paid but available at negotiated group rates.

Employees electing the HDHP plan receive an annual company contribution to their HSA of $750 for employee-only coverage or $1,500 for family coverage, deposited in two installments at the start of each half of the plan year.

## 4. Enrollment Periods
New employees must enroll in benefits within 30 days of their eligibility date; failure to enroll within this window results in a default of no coverage until the next open enrollment period, except where a qualifying life event applies. Open enrollment occurs annually each November, with elections taking effect January 1. Outside of open enrollment, employees may only change elections following a qualifying life event, such as marriage, divorce, birth or adoption of a child, or loss of other coverage, and must submit the change request within 30 days of the event.

## 5. Wellness Program
Employees enrolled in a Meridian medical plan may participate in the wellness program, which includes an annual biometric screening, a confidential health risk assessment, and access to a wellness reimbursement of up to $300 per year for eligible expenses such as gym memberships, fitness classes, or wearable fitness devices. Reimbursement requests are submitted through the same Expense Reimbursement process used for other company expenses (POL-EXP-200), with wellness receipts flagged accordingly.

## 6. Mental Health and Employee Assistance Program
All employees, regardless of medical plan enrollment, have access to Meridian's Employee Assistance Program (EAP), which provides confidential counseling sessions, financial and legal consultation referrals, and crisis support at no cost. The EAP is available to employees and their immediate household members and is not tied to enrollment in any medical plan.

## 7. Life and Disability Insurance
Meridian automatically provides basic life insurance coverage equal to one times annual base salary, and basic short-term and long-term disability coverage, to all full-time employees at no cost. Employees may purchase additional voluntary life insurance for themselves or dependents at group rates through payroll deduction, up to plan maximums.

## 8. Leave of Absence and Benefits Continuation
Employees on an approved leave of absence, including parental leave or medical leave, continue to receive employer-subsidized benefits on the same terms as active employees for the duration of the approved leave, provided the employee continues to pay their share of premiums. Employees on unpaid personal leave or sabbatical (see the Leave Policy, POL-LV-500) may continue coverage at the full unsubsidized group rate, paid directly to People Operations.

## 9. COBRA and Termination of Coverage
Coverage ends on the last day of the month in which employment ends. Departing employees and eligible dependents may continue medical, dental, and vision coverage under COBRA, at their own expense, for up to 18 months, or longer in certain qualifying circumstances. People Operations provides COBRA enrollment information within the legally required timeframe following termination.

## 10. Plan Changes
Meridian reserves the right to modify, replace, or discontinue any benefit plan described in this policy, subject to advance notice to employees and compliance with applicable law. Material changes are communicated during the annual open enrollment period wherever possible.

## 11. Questions
Employees with questions about eligibility, enrollment, or specific plan coverage should contact People Operations or refer to the detailed plan documents provided by Meridian's benefits carriers at the time of enrollment.
