# Meridian Analytics — Leave Policy

Meridian Analytics — Leave Policy

Document ID: POL-LV-500

Version: 1.0

Publication date: 1 March 2026

Owner: People Operations

## 1. Purpose and Scope
This policy summarizes the paid and unpaid leave entitlements available to full-time employees at Meridian Analytics. It applies globally, except where local law provides greater entitlements. Category-specific policies, such as the PTO Policy (POL-PTO-400), may contain additional detail; where a conflict exists, the more detailed category-specific policy governs on matters of accrual and mechanics.

## 2. Eligibility
All active full-time employees are eligible for the leave categories described below from their date of hire, except where a specific waiting period is noted for a particular category (for example, parental leave and sabbatical). Part-time employees receive pro-rated entitlements based on scheduled hours. Contractors and interns are not covered by this policy and should refer to their individual service agreement.

## 3. Vacation / Paid Time Off
Employees accrue Paid Time Off at a rate of 20 days per year, credited monthly. Up to 5 unused days may roll over into the following calendar year; any balance beyond that is forfeited on December 31, unless local law requires otherwise. Requests of 5 consecutive business days or fewer require manager approval only. Longer requests require both manager and People Operations approval, submitted at least two weeks in advance to allow for coverage planning.

## 4. Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 days of sick leave per year, credited at the start of the leave year rather than accrued monthly. Sick leave may be used for the employee's own illness or injury, or to care for an immediate family member who is ill. Unused sick leave does not roll over and is not paid out upon termination, except where required by local law. A doctor's note is required for absences exceeding three consecutive days.

## 5. Parental Leave
Employees who have completed at least six months of continuous service are eligible for up to 12 weeks of parental leave following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child. The first six weeks are paid in full; the remaining six weeks, if taken, are paid at 50% of base salary. Leave may be taken continuously or intermittently with manager approval, and should generally be completed within 12 months of the qualifying event. Employees should notify People Operations at least 30 days before an anticipated start date whenever the need for leave is foreseeable.

## 6. Bereavement Leave
Employees may take up to 5 paid days of bereavement leave following the death of an immediate family member, or up to 3 paid days for the death of an extended family member or close friend, at manager discretion. Additional unpaid bereavement leave may be requested and is evaluated case by case by People Operations.

## 7. Jury Duty and Civic Leave
Employees summoned for jury duty, or subpoenaed as a witness in a legal proceeding unrelated to a personal matter, receive fully paid leave for the duration of the service upon presenting the summons or subpoena to People Operations. Employees released early on any given day are expected to return to work if practical.

## 8. Military Leave
Leave for military service, including training and active duty, is provided in accordance with applicable law. Specific pay continuation and reinstatement rights vary by jurisdiction and length of service. Employees should contact People Operations as soon as orders are received so that applicable entitlements can be confirmed.

## 9. Sabbatical and Unpaid Personal Leave
Employees with at least five years of continuous service may apply for an unpaid sabbatical of up to 90 days for personal, educational, or volunteer purposes. Requests require Department Head and People Operations approval, submitted at least 60 days in advance. Health benefits continue during an approved sabbatical, though PTO and sick leave do not accrue during this period. Shorter unpaid personal leave, up to 30 days, may also be granted at manager discretion for circumstances not covered by another category.

## 10. Requesting Leave
All leave requests are submitted through the HR portal to ensure accurate and consistent recordkeeping. Urgent cases, such as bereavement or jury duty, may be reported verbally or by email to a manager first, with formal logging in the portal to follow within a few business days. Managers may not deny statutory leave, such as jury duty or protected parental leave, and should escalate uncertainty about a request to People Operations rather than denying it outright.

## 11. Return to Work
Employees returning from leave exceeding two consecutive weeks should notify their manager at least three business days beforehand. Employees returning from parental or approved medical leave are guaranteed reinstatement to their same or an equivalent role, consistent with applicable law.

## 12. Questions
Direct any questions about eligibility, entitlement, or the request process under this policy to People Operations.
