Carbon-Reduction-Plan > Emissions reduction targets

Coforge is a fast-growing IT services firm, and the business has grown over 100% in the past 4 years. The growth in the business has led to increase of headcount and business operations across the globe. As an organisation, we acknowledge that this could lead to increase in the number of absolute emissions, directly proportionate to the increase in headcount and business operations. However, as an organisation we are committed to assessing our carbon emissions and to relatively reduce them by 20% by 2030 (from our baseline year) whilst we grow as a firm. We target to reach net-zero by 2050 in our global operations. We aim to achieve our taking-by- taking active steps through carbon sequestration and use of renewable energy technologies such as wind & solar, hydroelectricity, and other renewable energy sources.

India Location - Since 2017 when the baseline emission numbers were assessed, there has been a continuous focus on emission reduction and focused interventions to ensure that the scope of emissions is controlled despite the growth of our business operations. Several of our pilot carbon reduction initiatives were conducted at our India operations.

International Locations (including the U.K.)- In 2022, we conducted emission assessment for all international business operations, including the United Kingdom. We are looking to undertake multiple initiatives to control these emissions, such as diminishing the need for air-travel where possible, given that it is a significant contributor to our emissions for international locations. We are continually assessing our operations for consolidation of resources to reduce the scale of negative impact on the environment.

# Single-use-Plastic-free-Policy copy
Single-use Plastic-free Policy
Review Date – 10th March 2026

This Policy applies to:

- All Coforge Limited offices, campuses, and operational sites across India, including all subsidiaries in India.

- All employees, contract staff, vendors, facility management teams, cafeteria partners, and on-site service providers operating within Coforge premises.

- All procurement of packaging, office supplies, pantry/cafeteria items, promotional materials, and catering items that may contain single-use plastics.

- All events and corporate activities organized by Coforge within Coforge premises (excluding events only sponsored by Coforge where operational control is not with Coforge).

- All waste handling and disposal managed internally or through external partners (external waste partners must comply with SUP-free requirements).

Operational Control & Third-Party Locations:

- This Policy is applicable within Coforge premises/boundary and where Coforge has operational control.

- At client sites or third-party venues, Coforge teams shall follow the intent of this Policy to the extent feasible, while complying with client/venue rules.

Single-use-Plastic-free-Policy copy > Purpose

Coforge recognizes that single-use plastics (SUPs)—such as disposable bags, cups, containers, cutlery, and packaging—create environmental risks due to persistent waste and leakage into land and water bodies. This Policy establishes Coforge’s commitment to reduce SUPs at source, enable reuse, and transition to responsible alternatives across operations in India.

Single-use-Plastic-free-Policy copy > Definitions

- Single-Use Plastic (SUP): Any plastic item designed to be used once (or for a very short period) before disposal (e.g., cutlery, cups, straws, stirrers, polystyrene foodware, balloon sticks, thin carry bags, shrink wrap used for internal logistics).

- Reusable: Durable items intended for multiple uses (e.g., metal cutlery, ceramic/glass cups, refillable dispensers).

- Recyclable: Products that can be recycled by available local recycling systems.

- Compostable: Products that can be composted in available local composting systems (where such systems exist).

- Event: Any planned gathering organized by Coforge within Coforge premises (meetings, workshops, celebrations, conferences, programs).

- Reuse-First: Reusable items are the default; disposables are permitted only under an approved exemption.

Single-use-Plastic-free-Policy copy > Goal

Coforge commits to progressively eliminating single-use plastics across all India sites by prohibiting the use, distribution, sale, and procurement of identified SUP items within Coforge premises, except where legally required or operationally unavoidable under an approved exemption.

This will be achieved through:

- Elimination at source (avoid SUP introduction into operations).

- Supplier transition to sustainable alternatives with appropriate accreditations/claims.

- Circularity in operations by prioritizing reuse and responsible material management.

Single-use-Plastic-free-Policy copy > Goal > Elimination & Substitution (Mandatory Prohibition)

The purchase, use, distribution, and sale of the following SUP items is prohibited within Coforge premises (unless approved under exemption):

- Plastic cutlery (fork/spoon/knife)

- Plastic plates, cups, and glasses

- Straws and stirrers

- Thermocol / EPS decorations and foodware

- Plastic wrapping / packing films used for internal operations

- Plastic carry bags below the minimum thickness prescribed under applicable Central/State rules (whichever is stricter)

Replacement rule: Reusables are the first preference; where reuse is not feasible, only locally recyclable or compostable alternatives may be used.

# Water-Management-Policy
Water Management Policy
Review Date – 10th August 2026

Coforge Limited (hereafter referred to as “Coforge”) is a global digital services and business solutions provider with in-depth domain expertise that specializes in selected industry verticals. The company's strategy is rooted in providing capability at scale with a focus on emerging technologies, building partnerships, client centricity, and creating a sustainable and responsible world.

Water is a shared and increasingly stressed natural resource that is critical to communities, ecosystems, and the continuity of Coforge’ s own operations. While Coforge, as an information technology services organization, is not a water-intensive business, the company recognizes its responsibility to manage water use across its facilities and value chain in a manner that is efficient, transparent, and protective of shared water resources, particularly in the water- stressed geographies in which several of its campuses and offices are located.

This Water Policy sets out Coforge's approach to responsible water stewardship. It builds on, and provides greater specificity to, the water-related commitments already articulated in Coforge's Environmental Sustainability Policy, including the company's ambition to become “Water Positive by 2040”, and establishes the principles, commitments, objectives, targets, and governance mechanisms that will guide Coforge in achieving this ambition. Coforge defines “Water Positive” as replenishing more water than is consumed across owned operations through a combination of water conservation, recycling, rainwater harvesting, watershed restoration, and community water stewardship initiatives.

The Policy aims to build trust with stakeholders, strengthen resilience against water-related risks, and ensure that Coforge's services and facilities are managed in an environmentally sound and responsible manner.

Water-Management-Policy > Responsible Water Use and Conservation

- Drive responsible management of water through conservation and efficient use across all delivery centers of Coforge, with the objective of reducing freshwater consumption and withdrawal intensity over time.

- Prioritize water sourcing based on a hierarchy that favors harvested rainwater, recycled water, and other sustainable alternative sources before increasing dependence on freshwater sources wherever feasible.

- Adopt water-efficient fixtures, low-flow sanitary fittings, smart water metering, and leak-detection systems to minimize water wastage across owned and significant leased premises.

- Over the next two years, Coforge will undertake water balance assessments for major facilities to understand sources, uses, and losses of water and identify opportunities for reduction and substitution with recycled or alternative water sources.

- Coforge intends to establish and periodically review near-term (3-5 year) and long-term (through 2040) water conservation targets, aligned with its broader Environmental Sustainability Policy commitments.

Water-Management-Policy > Water Risk Assessment

Coforge recognizes that water-related risk, including physical scarcity, flooding, declining water quality, and regulatory change, varies significantly by geography and can affect business continuity, cost of operations, and the communities in which Coforge's facilities are located:
